Question & Answer Submission in SEO is an off-page SEO technique where you participate in Q&A platforms by answering relevant questions related to your business, niche, or services. The goal is to provide valuable answers and include a link back to your website to drive traffic and build authority.

✅ Benefits:

Increases website traffic

Builds backlinks

Enhances brand visibility and trust

Positions you as an expert in your niche

🔍 Popular Q&A Platforms:

Quora

Reddit (specific subreddits)

Stack Exchange

Answer.com

ask.com

linkedin.com

Local forums
