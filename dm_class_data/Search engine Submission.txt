What is DA/PA 

(DA/PA Checker Moz Extension)

In the context of Search Engine Optimization (SEO), DA stands for Domain Authority and PA stands for Page Authority. These are metrics, developed by Moz, that assess the strength of a website or individual page in terms of its potential to rank in search engine results. 

Domain Authority (DA) is a score that reflects the overall strength and trustworthiness of an entire website. It's a composite score ranging from 1 to 100, with higher scores indicating a more authoritative and reputable domain. 

Page Authority (PA) is a score that reflects the ranking potential of a specific page on a website. It's also measured on a scale of 1 to 100, and higher scores suggest a page has a greater chance of ranking well in search results. 

DA and PA are not official ranking factors used by search engines like Google, but they can be useful indicators of a website's or page's overall strength and potential. Understanding and improving these metrics can be part of a broader SEO strategy aimed at increasing visibility in search engine results pages.
